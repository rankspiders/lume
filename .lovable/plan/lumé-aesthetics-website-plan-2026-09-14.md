# Lumé Aesthetics Website Plan

## Goal
Create a sleek, editorial website for Lumé Aesthetics that feels bespoke rather than template-generated. The site will preserve the selected ivory-and-copper direction, use the supplied Lumé identity, and balance spa warmth with credible advanced-aesthetics presentation.

## Pages and experience
- **Home:** Full-screen image-led introduction, Lumé tagline, signature treatment categories, selected rituals, advanced aesthetics, Beauty Atelier, brand philosophy, appointment prompts, and Mohali contact details.
- **Treatments:** A polished, easy-to-scan menu organized into Skin & Body Rituals, Beauty Atelier, Advanced Aesthetics, and Add-ons. Use the uploaded treatment names, descriptions, durations, and prices without inventing unsupported claims or prices.
- **About:** Lumé philosophy and space, with a clearly reserved practitioner/team area that avoids fabricated credentials.
- **Contact:** Location, phone, email, appointment call-to-action, and practical visit information sourced from the current site.

## Visual direction
- Carry forward the selected **Editorial Ivory & Copper** composition: immersive centered opening, generous spacing, refined serif typography, warm ivory, copper/rose-gold, and charcoal.
- Use the supplied Lumé logo prominently and derive the browser icon from the uploaded brand mark.
- Create a cohesive set of original, high-end treatment and interior images that match Lumé’s warm editorial atmosphere.
- Use restrained transitions and subtle image movement; avoid rounded-card grids, excessive gradients, decorative blobs, generic icons, and stock-template phrasing.
- Ensure every section remains polished and readable on phones and larger screens.

## Content and conversion
- Feature the verified tagline: “Beautiful skin. Confidence within.”
- Preserve the treatment language from the uploaded Lumé menu, lightly editing only for web readability and consistency.
- Lead advanced services with consultation-first wording and avoid medical or practitioner claims not supported by supplied materials.
- Make booking consistently accessible from the header and key page moments. Until a booking URL is provided, the action will use the verified phone contact rather than a fake scheduling flow.
- Include the verified Mohali address, phone number, and email. Placeholder claims from the existing site will not be reused.

## Technical details
- Build with the existing TanStack Start and Tailwind setup.
- Add dedicated routes for Home, Treatments, About, and Contact, with working navigation and unique page metadata.
- Define all brand colors, typography, borders, and interaction states through the shared design system.
- Add the uploaded brand artwork through the project asset flow and a real SVG favicon.
- Add accessible labels, useful image descriptions, semantic headings, keyboard-friendly navigation, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Validate the finished site at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, all page links, typography, image loading, and appointment/contact actions.

## Content assumptions
- Business details currently treated as verified: Unit 928–930, Imperial Tower CP-67, Mohali; +91 98723 25444; info@lumeaesthetics.co.
- Social links, opening hours, practitioner names/credentials, and a dedicated booking URL will be omitted or clearly reserved until supplied.
